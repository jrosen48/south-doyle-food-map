South-Doyle 2-Mile Food Map

OSM food locations within a 2-mile straight-line radius of South-Doyle Middle School, Knoxville TN
Categories: ·
About this data

Candidate locations from OpenStreetMap, filtered to a 2-mile (3,218.7 m) straight-line radius on the WGS84 ellipsoid around a campus reference point at 3900 Decatur Road. This is not travel distance, and the map is not a complete business directory.

Solid dots carry an OSM food category; hollow dots (pharmacies, gas stations, dollar stores, bars) are potential food sellers that need verification. No location has been field-checked for current operation.

Buildings and areas are placed at the bounding-box center OSM returns, so some positions are approximate. Records flagged as possible duplicates share a name within 40 m.
